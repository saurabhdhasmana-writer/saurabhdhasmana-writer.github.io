INITIATIVE 01

Building a Unified Documentation System from 70+ Product Kits

Challenge: Documentation for more than 70 product kits was spread across multiple documents and locations, making updates difficult to maintain and information harder to find.

What I did: Restructured the information architecture and consolidated product content into a single HTML-based manual with reusable content, solution-based navigation, visual installation guidance, troubleshooting, and centralized resources.

Impact: Reduced documentation turnaround time by approximately 60–70% while improving consistency, maintainability, and information access.

INITIATIVE 02

Automating Legacy FrameMaker-to-DITA Migration

Challenge: Migrating a large collection of legacy FrameMaker documents manually would require significant time alongside ongoing responsibilities. Hiring temporary resources would also increase project costs.

What I did: Assessed the manual effort, researched automated solutions, identified STILO Migrate, and initiated discussions with the vendor. I worked with the company to define customized migration rules aligned with the required DTD and stylesheets, then coordinated the conversion to structured DITA content.

Impact: Completed the migration in approximately two weeks, avoiding the need for additional temporary resources and significantly reducing migration effort.
