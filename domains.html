04 / DOMAINS

Different products.
Different worlds.
Same goal: clarity.

A domain is more than an industry label. It shapes the users, workflows, terminology, risks, and documentation decisions a writer has to understand.

01

Healthcare & Clinical Research

Experience: Healthcare software, medical devices, clinical-trial software, and documentation supporting complex workflows.

Learn: Understand the roles, regulated processes, data flows, and terminology before documenting the product.

02

Unified Communications

Experience: Product documentation for hardware and software across a Unified Communications portfolio, including work covering more than 80 products.

Learn: Product architecture, installation, configuration, interoperability, and customer workflows are central to useful documentation.

03

Aviation

Experience: Flight operations documentation, crew training material, quality system manuals, and Electronic Flight Bag deployment.

Learn: Accuracy, currency, compliance, and operational context are critical.

04

Enterprise Software

Experience: Documentation across enterprise hardware and software projects, Agile and Waterfall environments, and globally distributed teams.

Learn: Stakeholder mapping, user journeys, release processes, and technical context help turn complex systems into usable information.

Future versions of this section will become a small learning resource: what the domain is, how the products work at a high level, what I documented, what writers need to know, and where readers can learn more from public sources.
