AI INITIATIVE 01

AI-Powered Release Notes & Documentation Automation

Challenge: Release documentation requires writers to analyze engineering tickets, draft customer-facing notes, identify affected documentation, recommend updates, and create Jira tickets across recurring release cycles.

What I built: An AI-assisted workflow using Claude Enterprise, application-specific Projects, reusable Skills, documentation impact analysis, suggested content updates, and command-driven Jira ticket creation.

Impact: Improved productivity and operational efficiency by more than 40%, shortened documentation turnaround times, and increased output while maintaining quality.
